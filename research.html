OPEN METHODOLOGY

Understand the math.

Every figure on this dashboard comes from a small set of explicit calculations.

01

Normal P/E

For each valid observation in the selected window, divide price by positive trailing twelve-month EPS. Exclude ratios below 2 or above 100, then take the median.

normal P/E = median(price ÷ TTM EPS)
02

Fair value

Multiply each period's trailing EPS by the selected window's normal P/E. A period with zero or negative EPS has no fair-value point.

fair value = TTM EPS × normal P/E
03

Margin of safety

Compare the latest price with the latest calculated fair value. Negative values mean the price is above the benchmark.

1 − latest price ÷ fair value
04

Growth & return

EPS growth uses the first positive EPS and latest positive EPS in the window. The scenario compounds EPS for five years, applies the selected exit P/E, and annualizes price change.

(future price ÷ current price) ^ (1 ÷ 5) − 1
05

The earnings line

The shaded orange area is trailing EPS times 15, a classic benchmark for a fairly valued business. The green area underneath is the dividend at the same multiple, the share of earnings paid out to owners. Switch to Normal P/E to shade using the company's own historical multiple instead.

earnings line = TTM EPS × 15
06

Where real data comes from

Month-end closing prices come from Cboe, with the current month at the latest quote (about 15 minutes delayed). Diluted EPS and dividends per share come from the company's own 10-K and 10-Q filings via SEC EDGAR. When a filing restates earlier per-share figures by a clean ratio, Lettuce treats it as a stock split and restates older figures to today's share basis. Quarterly values are summed into trailing twelve-month figures and interpolated between quarter ends.

TTM EPS = sum of last four quarters
